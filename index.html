The night the current went

"Current pochu!" is what a whole Tamil street shouts in the same second the power dies. It is also the first thing that happens in this game.

Everyone blames Lineman Murugesan, but he swears the fuse is fine: the lines are empty, as if something drank the current with a straw. So the kids of Kamarajar Street pick up their torches and go to find out.

Along the way they keep meeting minminis, tiny spark-bugs carrying sips of electricity uphill, and something small, bright and frightened that is always one step ahead of them.

What you do

  • Light the town

    Shine your torch at the minminis and they follow you. Walk three to a dead streetlight and it wakes up for everyone.

  • Play together or alone

    Up to four friends online with a join code, or solo with no internet. Battery the dog fills in when you need a second pair of paws.

  • Six chapters, one story

    The town, the paddy fields, an abandoned cinema, a goods yard, a windy ridge and the old dam. Each one adds a new kind of puzzle.

  • Nobody gets hurt

    No lives, no game over. Bandicoots scatter your minminis, ghosts send you back to the gate, and you try again.

  • Eight kids to play

    Kavin, Abdul, Arul, Muthu, Yazhini, Mercy, Nila and Kayal. Pick one and your friends see who you are.

  • Plays anywhere

    Windows, Linux, Android and the browser all join the same game. Keyboard, controller or touch.

The chapters

  1. Bazaar Blackout. Fourteen dead streetlights and four fuses to earn: lost glasses, a runaway goat, melting ice and six cricket balls.
  2. The Pump-set. A maze of bunds through flooded paddy, three sluice valves on a timer, and a scarecrow wearing something that is not his.
  3. Last Show at Raja Talkies. Three film reels, a projector, three mirrors, and a ghost that freezes in torchlight.
  4. Goods Yard. Three shunting engines that cannot see you, four signal lanterns, and a lever puzzle.
  5. Kaatthaadi Hills. Gusts of wind, crumbling ledges, and three windmills to set turning.
  6. The Powerhouse. One generator, eight minminis, and a small friend who needs to get home.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source, with no ads and no purchases.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Solo play needs no internet. Battery the dog follows you, and a whistle tells him to stay put when a puzzle needs something heavy in one place.

How do I play online with friends?

One of you chooses Host Online and gets a six-character code. Everyone else types that code on the title screen. Windows, Linux, Android and browser players can all be in the same game.

Windows says "unknown publisher". Is that normal?

Yes. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

How do I install the Android APK?

Download it on your phone and open it. Android will ask you to allow installing from that source the first time.

Does it save my progress?

Yes, on the device of whoever hosts, and guests keep a copy too, so anyone in the group can host next time.

What are the controls?

WASD or arrow keys to move, E to talk and use, Q to call Battery, M for the map, Esc for the menu. Every key can be changed in the settings. Controllers and touch screens work too.
