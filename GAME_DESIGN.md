# Current Pochu!

*A power-cut adventure for 1–4 friends.*

"Current pochu!" is what a whole Tamil street shouts at the same moment the power dies.
That shout is the first thing that happens in this game.

- **Genre:** casual co-op adventure, top-down 2D pixel art
- **Players:** 1–4 online (best with 2–3). Solo is fully playable.
- **Length:** about 90 minutes of story, plus side-jobs in the town
- **Platforms:** Windows and Linux, playing together online with a join code
- **Tone:** funny, warm, a little spooky in the dark. Nobody dies, nothing is killed.

## The story

**Minnalpatti**, a small town at the foot of the hills. Tonight is the district cricket
final and the whole town is crowded around televisions. At 7:59 PM, with the last overs
about to start, every light in town goes out.

Everyone blames Lineman Murugesan, the Electricity Board's one overworked lineman. But he
swears the fuse is fine. This is not a normal power cut: the lines are *empty*. Something
is drinking the current.

The kids of Kamarajar Street grab their torches and follow the dead wires out of town:
through the bazaar, across the paddy fields, into the abandoned Raja Talkies cinema, over
the railway yard, up the windmill hills, and finally to the old dam powerhouse.

Along the way they keep finding **minminis**: tiny spark-bugs, glowing like fireflies,
carrying sips of electricity uphill.

**The twist.** At the dam they find who the minminis are feeding: **Minnal**, a baby
lightning bolt that fell out of last week's storm. It is scared of the dark and too weak
to jump back to the clouds, so it has been pulling every light in the valley towards
itself like a child hoarding nightlights.

**The ending.** You do not fight it. You get the whole town to help: every chapter's
restored line is switched to send one giant jolt up to the dam, and you launch Minnal
home. The lights come back across the map in a wave, the town catches the final over,
and from that night on, lightning over Minnalpatti never strikes anything.

### The running clock

A battery radio follows the cricket final between chapters ("34 needed off 18…").
It is the reason everybody in town is desperate, and the joke that keeps the pace up.

## How it plays

**Core loop:** explore in the dark with your torch → solve a co-op puzzle → relight a
streetlight → that part of the map stays lit for good.

- **Torch.** Your light cone reveals the world. It also attracts minminis and scares off
  pests (bandicoots that steal batteries, crows, the "ghost" in the cinema).
- **Minminis.** Lead them with your light into lamps, fuse boxes and motors to power things.
- **Carry and throw.** Fuses, batteries, crates, and each other's dropped items.
- **Streetlights.** Checkpoints. If you get spooked you pop back to the last lit one. No
  lives, no game over.
- **Whistle.** One button to call your friends ("Dei! Inga vaa!") with a ping on screen.

Each chapter adds exactly one new toy, so the game never repeats itself for long.

## The world

Minnalpatti is one connected map. The town is the hub; each chapter's area opens when
its power line is restored, and stays open to revisit. Dark areas are unexplored,
lit areas are finished: the map itself is the progress bar.

| # | Chapter | Place | New toy | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 7:59 PM | Kamarajar Street | Torch, streetlights | 5 |
| 1 | Bazaar Blackout | Bazaar street, tea stall, wedding hall | Minminis, fuses, battery thieves | 12 |
| 2 | The Pump-set | Paddy fields and canals | Sluice gates and rafts | 14 |
| 3 | Last Show at Raja Talkies | Abandoned cinema | Projector beam and mirrors; the "ghost" | 15 |
| 4 | Goods Yard | Railway yard | Trolleys, track switches, a train to dodge | 14 |
| 5 | Kaatthaadi Hills | Windmill ridge in a rising storm | Wind gusts, turbine blades, kites | 14 |
| 6 | The Powerhouse | Old dam | Everything together; launching Minnal | 15 |
| – | Final Over | The whole town | Lights return, free roam | 5 |

**Side-jobs in town** (optional, 15–20 more minutes): find Paati's glasses, catch the tea
stall's runaway goat, deliver the wedding hall's ice before it melts, and so on. Rewards
are cosmetic: torch colours, hats, a bell for Battery.

## Online play

- One player hosts and gets a short join code; friends enter it. No port forwarding.
- Friends can drop in and out at any time. The host's game keeps the progress.
- Puzzles adapt to the number of players; solo uses Battery the dog.
